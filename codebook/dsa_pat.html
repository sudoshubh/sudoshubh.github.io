The 16-week route

Four phases, one feedback loop

Do not solve randomly. Each phase has a purpose: build recognition, increase difficulty, simulate interviews, and close gaps.

Weeks 1–4
Foundation

Learn the templates

Target 35–40 easy and medium problems.

  • Arrays, hashing, strings, sliding window, two pointers
  • Prefix sums, binary search, linked lists, stack basics
  • Write a reusable template and complexity note for every pattern
Weeks 5–8
Core DSA

Move from recognition to execution

Target 40–45 medium problems.

  • Trees, BST, DFS, BFS, heaps, intervals, backtracking
  • For every problem: brute force, optimized idea, proof, complexity
  • One 45-minute timed set every week
Weeks 9–12
Advanced

Handle ambiguity and hard variants

Target 35–40 medium and selected hard problems.

  • Dynamic programming, graph algorithms, topological sort
  • Monotonic stack, union-find, shortest path, advanced binary search
  • Two mock interviews every week with a peer or interviewer platform
Weeks 13–16
Interview mode

Convert preparation into offers

Revise; do not keep expanding the list indefinitely.

  • Repeat missed problems after 1, 3, 7, and 14 days
  • Complete 8–12 timed mocks across arrays, graphs, trees, and DP
  • Prepare project deep dives, system design, OS, DBMS, and networking

Weekly execution

Use the same rhythm every week

Consistency beats occasional 12-hour sessions. The goal is reliable recall under pressure, not a large unsolved backlog.

Day Primary Work Output Time
Monday Learn one pattern and solve one easy problem. Template + invariant + complexity notes 90 min
Tuesday Solve two medium problems from the same pattern. Independent solutions before editorial review 2 hours
Wednesday Re-solve Monday's problem without notes. Spaced-repetition checkpoint 75 min
Thursday Mixed-pattern practice. Identify pattern within five minutes 2 hours
Friday Timed interview set. Two problems in 60–75 minutes 90 min
Saturday Mock interview and CS fundamentals. Communication feedback + one system-design topic 3 hours
Sunday Revision and error-log cleanup. Re-solve misses from the week 2 hours
